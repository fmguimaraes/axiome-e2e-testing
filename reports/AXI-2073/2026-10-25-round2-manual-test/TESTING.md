- There is no field linking a post-hoc run to its omnibus run, so the F2 warning means "the rule declares a prerequisite and nothing records it was met".
- c700a6bd still cites the ENT 059 snapshot while its chart covers 3 facets, hence the scope-mismatch warning: a data fix is owed.
